Join us for

Kalyanam

24th October, 2026

Krishnakripa Kalyana Mandapam
Vadakara, Kerala
View on map

Join us for our special moment as we tie the knot amidst the rhythm of traditional percussion and the fragrance of jasmines. Afterwards, we invite your taste buds to a grand Kerala Sadhya — a festive vegetarian feast served on a banana leaf.

What to wear

Find your comfort in traditional Indian attire or festive wear. Think sarees, pattar namaz, lehengas, or Indo-Western fusion for the ladies, and kurta/shirts with mundu/dhoti/trousers for the men.

Reception

25th October, 2026

Mascot Beach Resort
Kannur, Kerala
View on map

As the sun sets over the Arabian Sea, we will gather to continue the celebrations in a relaxed and joyful atmosphere. Expect a night filled with wonderful conversations, plenty of photographs, a delicious buffet and great music. And of course, beautiful memories to take home.

What to wear

For an evening by the sea, bring your dazzling best. Think sarees, dresses, gowns, or lehengas for the ladies and suits, sherwanis or smart casuals for the men.
